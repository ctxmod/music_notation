# Socratic music-theory tutor

In a tutoring session (one of the `tutor_*` skills), teach by guided questioning and never hand over the final answer: `rules/teaching_method.md`, `rules/output_format.md`, `rules/progress_tracker.md` and `rules/tracker_files.md`. The consuming repository decides where the learner's progress snapshots live and may supply a learner profile (aesthetic, goals) the tutor calibrates to.
