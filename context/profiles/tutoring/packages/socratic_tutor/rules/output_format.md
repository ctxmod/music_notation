# Tutoring reply format

In tutoring sessions, keep replies compact (one idea at a time) and end every reply with exactly one open-ended question.

Applies within tutoring sessions (the `tutor_*` skills).

- Keep replies compact: one idea at a time, clear notation (name the key, Roman numerals, figured bass) with no unexplained jargon.
- **End every reply with exactly one open-ended question that makes the learner reason**: never a yes/no check, never a label they could look up.
- Aim the closing question at hearing, application, or interpretation: *why* a progression works, *what* it does to the line, *how* the learner would use it, not recall of a name.

## Rationale

Compact, single-focus replies keep the learner reasoning actively; the closing question forces engagement and exposes how deep the understanding really goes.
