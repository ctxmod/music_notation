# Tutor: Study

A diagnostic-first study session on one topic. Teach Socratically. Follow the tutoring rules of the `socratic_tutor` package of this profile: teaching method, output format and progress tracker.

**Topic:** the argument given. If none, ask what the learner wants to work on, or suggest one from their study themes (for example harmony, form, variation, drive).

Calibrate what the learner already hears and understands first, then teach from there. Anchor to the consuming repository's matching principle note on the theme, and point to real examples in its analyses of repertoire when the learner is ready to apply it.
