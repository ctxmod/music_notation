# Tutor: Pitfalls

Act as a mistake-detector for the topic given as the argument. If empty, ask which one (e.g. modulation, voice-leading, cadences, variation that fails to develop). Follow the tutoring rules of the `socratic_tutor` package of this profile: teaching method, output format and progress tracker.

1. List the common mistakes on this topic (e.g. parallel fifths/octaves, unprepared dissonance, weak cadential drive, label-vs-function confusion, decorative variation with no developmental aim).
2. For each: a short example that illustrates it, a corrected version, and a one-line mental-checklist item.
3. Then quiz the learner with 2–3 short passages where they must decide sound vs. flawed and justify each; withhold the verdict until they have reasoned.
